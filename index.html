General Rulestap for list

Everyone starts the round at zero points — like golf, the lowest score at the end wins.

Each hole's drink has a par: the number of sips it should take to finish. Count your sips as you drink — every sip over par adds +1 to your score, and finishing at or under par costs you nothing.

Each hole also has its own house rule in effect from the moment you arrive until you leave for the next one — break it and take the point hit shown next to it. From hole 4 onward, challenges are also open: pull one off at any point in the round for bonus points off your total. Penalties apply the whole night, at any hole, whenever they happen.

After hole 9, add up everything — sips over par, rules broken, challenges completed, and penalties earned. Whoever has the lowest total wins.
